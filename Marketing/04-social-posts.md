## 3. Customer approval

### LinkedIn

Your customer controls the AWS connection.

They open your installation link. They review the resources, AWS cost estimate, and requested permissions. Then they create the connector in their own AWS console.

They own the resources and pay AWS directly. They do not need a Deployz account.
