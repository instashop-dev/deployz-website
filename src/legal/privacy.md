Deployz is operated by Thalia Technologies Private Limited, Mumbai, India ("Deployz", "we", "us"). We respect your privacy. This policy explains the information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

## 1. Information we collect

- Account information: Your name, email address, organization details, and account preferences.

- Repository and deployment information: Information from repositories and integrations you authorize, potentially including source code, configurations, build artifacts, logs, and deployment metadata.

- Technical information: IP addresses, browser details, service activity, diagnostics, and cookies.

- Billing information: Subscriptions, billing details, and payment status. Payments may be processed by external providers.

- Support information: Information you provide when contacting us.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to operate Deployz, connect integrations, analyze and build applications, manage deployments, process payments, provide support, maintain security, improve services, and meet legal obligations. Where applicable, processing relies on contractual necessity, legitimate interests, legal obligations, or consent.

## 3. Your applications and AWS infrastructure

Deployz helps you deploy applications into AWS accounts you control. Your deployed applications, databases, and storage operate in your AWS environment. You remain responsible for the data your applications collect and process. Deployz separately handles repository information, deployment settings, build artifacts, logs, and operational metadata as needed for its services. We do not claim ownership of your source code or application data.

## 4. AI-assisted functionality

Deployz may use AI services to analyze repositories and suggest deployment configurations. Relevant source code or configuration details may be processed by third-party AI providers where necessary. AI recommendations are used alongside programmatic checks and may be incomplete or inaccurate. We do not independently use private repositories to train our own general-purpose AI models. Third-party providers operate under their applicable data-handling terms.

## 5. Sharing information

We share information with providers that help operate Deployz, such as infrastructure, repository integration, AI, payment, communications, error-logging, and analytics providers. We may also disclose information where legally required, to protect our services, or in a legitimate business transfer. We do not sell personal information.

## 6. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information. No online system is completely secure. Customers remain responsible for securing their AWS accounts, applications, permissions, and credentials.

## 7. Retention and deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain security, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Following account closure, information is deleted or anonymized in accordance with our operational processes and applicable requirements. Some data may remain temporarily in backups or where legally permitted or required. Closing a Deployz account does not automatically remove resources in your AWS account.

## 8. Cookies and analytics

We may use cookies and similar technologies for authentication, security, functionality, and analytics. Where required, we provide consent choices. You can also manage cookies through your browser.

## 9. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction or deletion of, or a copy of your personal information; object to or restrict certain processing; or withdraw consent where applicable. We may verify your identity before responding.

## 10. International processing

Deployz and its providers may process information outside your country. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

## 11. Updates

We may update this policy. We will revise the date above and provide notice where required.
